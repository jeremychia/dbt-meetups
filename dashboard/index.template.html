What the community is talking about

Every talk is tagged with topics, then rolled into one of 8 categories by its primary topic. Click a slice or legend row to see its topics and sample talks. All-time; narrowed by your chapter and any chart filter below, not by the window slider.

How this is computedEach talk's topic list comes from AI-assisted enrichment of the meetup description; the first topic listed is treated as the "primary" topic and mapped to one of 8 categories via a fixed lookup table. A talk is counted once, under its primary category only.

What counts as active / at-risk / dormant Active: at least one event in the trailing 12 months. At-risk: active, but no event in the last 9 months (trending toward dormant). Dormant: no event in the trailing 12 months.
